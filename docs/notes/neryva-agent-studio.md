# Neryva Agent Studio - Product Definition

## 1. Product Summary

Neryva Agent Studio is the professional customer-service and brand-representation layer for enterprise AI.

It exists for organizations that want an AI system to speak and behave like part of the business, not like a general-purpose model. The product turns a customer-selected model into a controlled customer service agent, brand assistant, or workflow assistant that stays on topic, follows business rules, and represents the company professionally.

The customer chooses the model that fits their budget and technical preferences. Neryva then provides the layer in between the model and the customer experience. That layer is what gives the system discipline, brand fit, and service quality.

## 2. Strategic Positioning

Neryva Agent Studio should be positioned as the experience layer that makes enterprise AI useful in customer-facing and business-facing settings.

The major advantage is not the base model itself. The major advantage is the control layer that shapes how the chosen model behaves when it speaks to customers, employees, or partners.

The core message is:

```text
We build and operate AI agents that represent your business professionally.
```

A slightly broader version is:

```text
We build and operate professional AI agents that stay on-brand, on-scope, and useful.
```

## 3. The Problem It Solves

Many companies deploy an AI chatbot or customer service assistant directly on top of a general model. The result often feels unprofessional because the model responds to anything instead of staying inside the customer-service role.

Common failures include:

- answering unrelated topics such as programming, trivia, or general advice
- drifting away from the company brand voice
- ignoring company policies and support boundaries
- giving responses that sound fluent but are operationally wrong
- missing escalation or handoff moments
- failing to act like a real service representative
- creating a weak customer experience

Neryva Agent Studio exists to prevent that failure mode.

## 4. What The Product Does

Neryva Agent Studio designs, builds, and operates a professional AI service layer for a company.

That layer uses the customer's chosen model, the customer's business information, and the customer's workflow requirements to create an agent that can answer questions, guide users, support service requests, and escalate correctly when needed.

The product should be understood as a managed service for creating professional AI chatbots, customer service agents, and brand-aligned assistants.

## 5. Core Service Areas

### 5.1 Business Understanding And Scope

Define what the agent is allowed to do and what it must not do.

This includes:

- business objective definition
- service scope
- support boundaries
- escalation criteria
- customer-service role definition
- approved knowledge sources

### 5.2 Brand And Voice Design

Shape how the agent speaks so it reflects the company properly.

This includes:

- brand voice
- tone
- response style
- professionalism level
- approved phrasing patterns
- disallowed language patterns

### 5.3 Business Knowledge Integration

Use the customer's business information so the agent can answer real questions correctly.

This includes:

- company FAQs
- product and service information
- policies and procedures
- support knowledge
- structured business data
- approved documentation

### 5.4 Customer-Service Behavior Layer

Control how the selected model behaves in live conversations.

This includes:

- staying inside the service role
- answering only relevant business questions
- refusing or redirecting unrelated prompts
- keeping responses concise and useful
- following support priorities
- keeping the interaction professional

### 5.5 Guardrails And Escalation

Keep the agent aligned with business policy and service standards.

This includes:

- allowed and disallowed topics
- escalation triggers
- human handoff logic
- compliance controls
- response restrictions
- risk controls

### 5.6 Tool And Workflow Support

Allow the agent to support practical customer or internal workflows.

This includes:

- ticket routing
- status checks
- request capture
- form completion
- workflow initiation
- internal task assistance

### 5.7 Testing And Improvement

Verify that the agent behaves properly before and after launch.

This includes:

- response testing
- scope testing
- escalation testing
- brand-consistency review
- edge-case evaluation
- continuous refinement

### 5.8 Ongoing Operations

Keep the agent useful after it is deployed.

This includes:

- content updates
- business rule updates
- workflow updates
- performance review
- issue resolution
- behavior adjustments

## 6. Product Focus

### Primary Focus

#### Customer Service Agent

An agent that handles product questions, support triage, account guidance, and common customer requests.

#### Brand Assistant

An agent that represents the company on the website, in chat, or in support channels with a consistent professional tone.

### Secondary Use Cases

#### Internal Assistant

An agent that helps employees find information, complete internal tasks, and follow company procedures.

#### Workflow Agent

An agent that supports structured service operations such as routing requests, collecting details, and preparing next steps.

## 7. Customer Outcomes

The product should create measurable outcomes such as:

- more professional customer interactions
- fewer off-topic responses
- stronger brand consistency
- better support quality
- faster response times
- better escalation discipline
- lower manual workload for the support team
- improved internal assistance where the company chooses to extend the same system inward
- structured workflow support as a secondary operational benefit

## 8. What The Product Is Not

Neryva Agent Studio is not:

- a base model vendor
- a raw infrastructure product
- a generic chatbot template
- a prompt-only service
- a loose automation tool
- a replacement for business policy or human oversight

## 9. Service Boundary

The product should stay focused on the agent experience layer.

Neryva Agent Studio controls the behavior, voice, scope, and workflow fit of the agent. It does not replace the underlying model provider, and it does not replace the secure deployment layer.

If the customer needs help deploying or operating the model itself, that belongs in Neryva AI Deployment.

## 10. How This Fits The Company

Neryva Agent Studio is the most visible part of the company because it is the layer customers interact with directly.

Neryva AI Deployment provides the secure runtime foundation.

Together, the two products create a complete enterprise AI offering:

- Deployment makes the AI system run securely and reliably.
- Agent Studio makes the AI system behave professionally and stay on-brand.

## 11. Success Definition

Neryva Agent Studio is successful when it can clearly answer these questions for the customer:

- What can the agent answer?
- What must the agent avoid?
- What tone should the agent use?
- How does the agent stay professional?
- When should the agent escalate?
- How does the agent use the customer's business information?
- How does the agent remain aligned over time?

If those questions are answered clearly, the product is doing its job.
